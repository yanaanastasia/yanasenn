Auch die Arbeits- und Messekleidung mit Logo gehört dazu.At trade fairs, on client visits and as a thank-you, you need promotional items that fit the brand and that people enjoy using, for our own trade fairs as well as for partners worldwide. I select the items, handle branding and print files, order them and organise storage and shipping. Branded work and trade fair clothing is part of it too.
